# Lessons

Guided stories for a grown-up and an advanced 5–6 year old. Games stay as play.
Lessons teach, then send the child into the matching games.

## Shape

Do not build 63 extra quizzes. Build **21 lessons**: one per topic per level.

```
Lesson (21)
  Scene 1  →  Look, Name, sometimes Change  →  Game 1
  Scene 2  →  Look, Name, sometimes Change  →  Game 2
  Scene 3  →  Look, Name, sometimes Change  →  Game 3
  Hands-on (kitchen / toys / getting dressed)
```

Each lesson is 6–8 minutes. The child can stop after one scene. Games stay
unlocked; lessons never gate play.

| Level | Voice | What the child does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner | See the difference | Tap the picture that matches one clear contrast |
| Intermediate | Name the science word | See it first, then hear farther, friction, habitat, predict |
| Advanced | Follow the chain | This, so that: steeper → faster, if wet → boots |

Physics still leads. Engineering is woven in through Make & Test, not saved
for later. Matter, living things, and gentle computing round out the week.

## What to build first

All 21 lessons already run as grown-up scripts with tap checks and a jump into
the three games. Waves 1 and 2 are picture-first in the app.

**Wave 1 — picture-first now**

1. **Beginner Motion** — Go, Stop, Push, Pull
2. **Beginner Forces & Stuff** — Heavy, Float, Sticky
3. **Beginner Make & Test** — Steady, Ramp, Fit
4. **Intermediate Motion** — Farther, Faster, Downhill

**Wave 2 — a complete first week, picture-first now**

5. Beginner Light & Sound — Bright, Loud, High
6. Beginner Matter — Splash, Cold, Melt
7. Beginner Living Things — Alive, Plant, Hungry
8. Beginner Follow the Steps — First, Next, Again

A 5–6 year old can then walk every topic once.

**Wave 3 — intermediate physics and making**

9. Intermediate Forces & Stuff
10. Intermediate Light & Sound
11. Intermediate Make & Test
12. Intermediate Matter

**Wave 4 — remaining intermediate plus advanced physics**

13. Intermediate Living Things
14. Intermediate Follow the Steps
15. Advanced Motion
16. Advanced Forces & Stuff
17. Advanced Make & Test

**Wave 5 — remaining advanced**

18. Advanced Light & Sound
19. Advanced Matter
20. Advanced Living Things
21. Advanced Follow the Steps

### Why this order

- The product leads with physics. Motion and Forces come first.
- Beginner Motion and Forces already have hand-built scenes, so those lessons
  are the cheapest high-quality upgrades.
- Make & Test is pulled into Wave 1 because engineering is woven in, not saved
  for a later unit.
- Intermediate Motion is the first non-beginner lesson so the “science word”
  voice is designed before the rest of intermediate is drawn.
- Advanced waits until the child has the beginner contrasts and the
  intermediate words. A 5–6 year old should not start on levers and vapor.

## What not to do

- Do not clone each game as its own lesson. That is 63 near-duplicates.
- Do not gate games behind lessons.
- Do not add units, formulas, or force arrows.
- Do not start with Follow the Steps or Living Things. They belong, but physics
  leads.
- Do not write a long grown-up lecture. One blue caption, one tap, one
  sentence of science.
